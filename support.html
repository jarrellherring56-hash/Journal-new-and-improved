Common questions

How do I turn the AI features on or off?

Open Customize → AI & your data. Choose Off and nothing is sent to an AI service. Your entries still save, just without mood tags or AI replies. You can switch it back on anytime.

What does the AI see, and who runs it?

When AI features are on, the text you write, the questions you ask, and voice recordings from Talk are sent to OpenRouter (which routes text to Anthropic's Claude model) and Groq (voice-to-text). Photos are never sent. Full details are in the privacy policy.

How do I back up or export my journal?

In Customize, tap Backup (.json) for a full backup you can import later, or Export journal (.txt) for a readable copy.

How do I delete my account?

In Customize, scroll to Delete account and confirm. This permanently removes your login and every entry, photo and setting. It can't be undone, so export a backup first if you want one.

I forgot my password.

Email us from the address you signed up with and we'll help you reset it.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check Customize → Reminders is on, and that notifications for Lucid are allowed in your device's Settings. On iPhone, reminders for the web version need it added to your Home Screen first.

Does my journal sync between devices?

Yes. Sign in with the same email and your entries, settings and photos follow you. A second device picks up changes the next time it opens the app. Your PIN lock is set separately on each device.

I forgot my PIN.

The PIN only locks the app on one device; your journal itself is stored in your account. Email us and we'll walk you through getting back in without losing anything.
